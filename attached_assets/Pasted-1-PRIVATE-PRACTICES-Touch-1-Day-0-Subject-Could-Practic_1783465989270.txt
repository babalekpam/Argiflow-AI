Additional Recommendations
I would also make these campaign-level changes:
Use a softer CTA. "Would it make sense to talk?" often outperforms "Schedule a meeting."
Add credibility early. Note that you have a team of billing and practice management professionals with over 30 years OR 3 decades of experience working across many different specialties and have billed through all insurance companies.
You may consider using:
Revenue Opportunity Assessment
Revenue Cycle Health Check
Practice Revenue Review
Collections Performance Review
Revenue Leakage Analysis
These sound more strategic and executive-level rather than "billing audit,".
Test different subject lines, such as:
Could {{Practice Name}} be collecting more?
Quick question about your billing
One revenue question
Reducing revenue leakage
Are you collecting everything you've earned?
Is money being left on the table?
Worth a second opinion on your revenue cycle?
One final suggestion: if your primary goal is booking meetings, I would build this as a 7-touch omnichannel sequence (email + LinkedIn + voicemail + phone + SMS + email + breakup email). That approach typically outperforms an email-only cadence and is more consistent with current B2B outbound best practices.
